World Guide

Explore Veyra by place, people, history, faith, magic, technology, travel, and everyday life. This guide preserves the uncertainties people inside the world still argue about.

Begin with the world

The Known World

Aldara, Caelora, Tsukara, the Shattered Isles, the surrounding seas, and the Uncrossed Mists.

Public History

The Sundering, reconstruction, new crowns, expanding technology, and the modern Connected Age.

Peoples

Humans, Beastfolk, Dwarves, Elves, Demonkin, mixed heritage, and the difference between heritage and homeland.

Languages

Common dialects, Aelthiric, Khordic, Tharic, Karthic, Tsukari, scripts, and multilingual life.

Lands and nations

Aldara

Old kingdoms, mountain holds, clan realms, trade routes, and the Elderwood.

Caelora

A continent-scale stewardship nation joined by Arcrail, public institutions, and saltstone infrastructure.

Tsukara

A guarded island civilization built around the sea, precision craft, spiritual tradition, and controlled access.

Shattered Isles

Broken lands in the Shattered Sea where trade, ruins, danger, and discovery meet.

Power, belief, and change

Faiths

The Four, Aureon, Varesh, ancestors, spirits, local faiths, and competing interpretations of the sacred.

The Mark & Gift

Coming of Age, ancestral Marks, Fixed Gifts, Open Gifts, and the beginning of magical potential.

Magic

Mana, spellcasting, magical traditions, rituals, training, and the limits of supernatural power.

Technology

Different technological ages living side-by-side: engineering, Arcrail, ships, saltstone, and precision craft.

Living in Veyra

Travel

Roads, rail, sea routes, wayhouses, borders, communication, and the scale of the known world.

Daily Life

Family, education, work, food, housing, medicine, law, leisure, and surviving dangerous frontiers.

Calendar & Time

Sundering Reckoning, months, Turning Days, local traditions, clocks, and standard time.

Currency & Measures

Trade Silver, regional currencies, banking, tolls, and the standards that keep trade moving.
