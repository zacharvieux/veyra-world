Velis Heartlands

The Velis Heartlands are the fertile central and eastern river plains associated with national government, dense population, agriculture, education, finance, and food production.

The Velis Heartlands are the fertile central and eastern river plains associated with national government, dense population, agriculture, education, finance, and food production.

The region contains heavily engineered rivers, canals, reservoirs, irrigation, flood controls, and some of the busiest Arcrail routes in the country.

Heartlanders often regard functional public systems as the normal condition of civilized life.
