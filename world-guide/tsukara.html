Tsukara is a sprawling southern island empire and the principal homeland of the Demonkin.

It is a maritime civilization of major islands, agricultural islands, fishing communities, sacred sites, fortresses, naval stations, reefs, and countless smaller settlements.

Tsukarans travel widely as sailors, merchants, physicians, navigators, diplomats, shipwrights, and explorers.

Government

Tsukara is governed day to day by professional ministers, governors, magistrates, judges, naval officials, civil servants, and councils.

The Emperor or Empress occupies a different role: spiritual guide, moral authority, symbol of continuity, guardian of long-term responsibility, and final check against severe governmental failure or corruption.

A traditional principle summarizes the relationship:

The government governs.
The Emperor judges whether it is still worthy to govern.

Imperial intervention is uncommon, which is part of why it carries such weight.

Foreign Access

Foreigners are protected in approved ports and legal trade zones but do not possess an unrestricted right to travel everywhere in the islands.

Sacred sites, military facilities, navigation stations, protected communities, and dangerous areas may be restricted.

Tsukarans generally regard this as ordinary sovereignty rather than hatred of foreigners.

Technology

Tsukara is technologically sophisticated while relying much less heavily on saltstone than Caelora.

Major strengths include:

  • ship design;
  • redundant maritime engineering;
  • precision mechanics;
  • metallurgy;
  • hydraulics;
  • tide and wind power;
  • counterweights;
  • optics and lenses;
  • chronometers;
  • compasses and navigation;
  • surgery and medical instruments;
  • sanitation and antiseptic practice;
  • limited naval explosives and cannon.

The Mist Needle is a famous precision navigation instrument built around carefully balanced magnetic mechanisms. Near the Mists, its components can behave strangely. Tsukaran navigators have recorded those behaviors for generations.

Faith and Sacred Life

Tsukaran communities may honor sea, river, island, mountain, storm, household, and object spirits, as well as ancestors, guardians, local gods, and greater sacred beings.

The boundary between god, spirit, ancestor, and sacred presence is not always treated as a problem that requires one universal answer.

Old stories preserve figures often translated as a Lantern, a Veiled Woman, a Blooming Mother, and a Gatekeeper. Foreign scholars frequently compare them with the Four Old Gods.

The Naming of the Gift

The common Coming-of-Age observance is the Naming of the Gift.

A young person's Gift is publicly acknowledged, although a Fixed Gift may still take months or years to understand fully. An Open-Gift youth may be connected with a teacher.

The Final Voyage

Tsukaran tradition teaches that when an Emperor or Empress completes their stewardship, the sovereign eventually relinquishes earthly rule and sails into the Uncrossed Mists.

This sacred departure is called the Final Voyage.

Public tradition connects the practice with the first Emperor and with the ancient catastrophe surrounding the Sundering. The departed sovereign is traditionally understood to journey toward the divine homeland or heavens rather than simply being described as dead.

Crossing Through the White

Another ancient Tsukaran tradition is the Crossing Through the White.

Stories describe catastrophic seas, vast fleets, storms, divine guidance, monsters, and a homeland lost beyond or behind the White.

No single version is universally accepted as literal history.
