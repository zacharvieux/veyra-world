Durakhal

Durakhal is an independent realm of dwarven mountain Holds north of Tharakai.

Durakhal is an independent realm of dwarven mountain Holds north of Tharakai.

It is organized as a confederation rather than a single tightly centralized kingdom. Individual Holds maintain their own traditions and laws while cooperating on shared defense, trade, roads, tunnels, engineering standards, and disputes that cross Hold boundaries.

Durakhal is known for:

  • mining and metallurgy;
  • engineering and stonework;
  • tools, armor, locks, precision mechanisms, and heavy construction;
  • strong archival traditions;
  • runecraft;
  • obligations expressed through family, craft, contract, and Hold.

Three ideas are especially influential in many Durakhal communities:

Memory. Craft. Obligation.

A promise can create duties that outlive the person who first made it. A family's reputation may be built over generations. Repair is often treated as seriously as invention.

Durakhal exports metal, tools, engineering expertise, stonework, weapons, armor, and mineral wealth. Food, timber, textiles, medicine, and agricultural products are important imports.

Religion is decentralized. Ancestor traditions, household spirits, local gods, mountain practices, Morthain, Nyvara, and other older traditions all have followers.
