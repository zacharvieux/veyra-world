The Elderwood / Aelthirien

The Elderwood, called Aelthirien in elven tradition, is not a kingdom.

The Elderwood, called Aelthirien in elven tradition, is not a kingdom.

It has no conventional capital, monarch, national census, or fixed political border that can be drawn accurately on a map.

No reliable conventional map of its interior exists.

Paths change. Streams shift. Clearings appear or vanish. Journeys that were short in one season may take much longer in another. Elves navigate by relationships, signs, memory, environment, and living knowledge rather than a fixed road atlas.

Two ways of traveling safely into the deeper Elderwood are widely accepted:

  1. travel with an elf who knows the forest; or
  2. carry a genuine blessing recognized by the Old Gods.

At its spiritual heart stands Ilyratha, the Great Tree. Ilyratha is not regarded as a monarch or a god.

Elderwood communities are decentralized. Authority may belong to councils, elders, specialists, spiritual leaders, family representatives, or temporary assemblies depending on place and need.

Elven life in the Elderwood is associated with long memory, ecological knowledge, fine craft, medicine, magic, scholarship, and traditions of the Old Gods. Elves do harvest wood and hunt; the cultural distinction is usually one of relationship, stewardship, and waste rather than absolute prohibition.

Valemere maintains the strongest regular relationship with the Elderwood, but even friendly outsiders do not receive unrestricted access.
