Ardaren

Ardaren is a human-majority kingdom of plains, rivers, vineyards, horse country, farms, noble estates, market towns, and merchant interests.

Ardaren is a human-majority kingdom of plains, rivers, vineyards, horse country, farms, noble estates, market towns, and merchant interests.

It balances monarchy, powerful noble houses, urban commerce, guilds, agriculture, military traditions, and older local customs.

Its social structure is more traditional than Valemere's but substantially less rigid than Solvarra's.

A common underlying attitude is:

If you contribute, you belong.

That ideal does not erase class or prejudice. Noble families hold real influence, and nonhuman families may need to prove themselves more thoroughly in some communities. However, nonhumans can own land, join guilds, serve in the military, build wealth, and become established members of society.

Ardaren is especially known for horses, cavalry, grain, wine, leather, river trade, agriculture, and old noble traditions.

Aureon is widely worshipped, but local gods, seasonal customs, ancestors, harvest traditions, and older rural faiths remain strong.
