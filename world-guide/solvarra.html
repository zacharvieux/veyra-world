Solvarra

A wealthy southern kingdom where Crown and Church reinforce hierarchy, public order, military power, and Aureonite religious authority.

Solvarra is one of Aldara's most organized states and one of its most restrictive social orders.

Crown and Church

Solvarra is a fertile, wealthy, organized, martial, and highly structured southern kingdom where the Crown and Church of Aureon are deeply intertwined.

The Church influences education, marriage, morality, public ceremony, religious courts, and social status. The Crown manages war, taxation, noble duties, foreign relations, and national infrastructure.

Solvarran crown-and-church institutions began taking recognizable form during the Age of New Crowns after the Sundering.

Order, Status, and Human Primacy

Society strongly values lineage, family reputation, religious standing, civic obedience, military service, land, public respectability, and social order.

Nonhuman communities exist, especially in trade cities and border regions, but may face heavier fees, guild restrictions, limitations on office, marriage restrictions, suspicion, and additional legal scrutiny.

Many outsiders experience the system as severe. Many people raised inside it experience the same structure as stability, predictability, and safety.

Aureon

Aureon is especially powerful in Solvarra. Public titles include Father of Man, Perfect Form, First King, Lord of Human Flame, and Unbroken Light.

The Church teaches order, hierarchy, purpose, civilization, obedience, and divine rule; some traditions explicitly teach human primacy. Aureonite practitioners are associated with genuine supernatural effects involving healing, light, courage, wards, strength, resistance to fear, and truth-seeking.

The existence of miracles does not settle every theological argument about what those miracles prove.

Language and Education

Solvarran Common is formal and strongly shaped by titles, religion, hierarchy, and older vocabulary.

Education is heavily influenced by Church and Crown. Children commonly learn reading, doctrine, history, arithmetic, and civic duty. Higher education is concentrated in church institutions, noble academies, military schools, and guilds.

The Church promotes literacy because doctrine, law, and administration benefit from readers, though access to advanced education remains unequal.

Roads and Foreign Travel

Solvarra maintains unusually disciplined military and tax roads. These roads support administration, taxation, troop movement, and the movement of goods across a wealthy agricultural state.

Foreigners and nonhuman travelers receive greater scrutiny than in places such as Valemere. Ordinary social knowledge includes understanding that openly insulting the Church can be dangerous.

Printing, News, and Law

The Church uses movable-type printing aggressively for doctrine, law, prayer books, education, and approved histories. Printed news exists but is subject to heavier religious and political oversight than in Valemere.

Solvarra possesses formal Crown and Church jurisdictions. Church officers may have authority over religious offenses that an ordinary civil watch does not.

Food, Clothing, and Daily Status

Solvarra is one of Aldara's major agricultural regions. Temperate staples include bread, porridge, beans, roots, cheese, preserved meat, river fish, fruit, ale, cider, and wine where climate permits.

Formal clothing tends to communicate rank and propriety clearly. Public behavior, family standing, religious reputation, and visible respectability all matter in a culture where hierarchy is expected to be legible.

Money, Tax, and Time

Solvarran coinage uses coppers for small trade, silver rays for ordinary commerce, and high-value crowns. The eight-rayed Aureonite sun appears prominently on official coinage.

The Crown and Church may both possess fiscal claims depending on jurisdiction.

Solvarra uses SR for international diplomacy but also maintains an Aureonite liturgical calendar organized around feast days, royal observances, and Church cycles. Formal documents commonly include both civil date and regnal year.
