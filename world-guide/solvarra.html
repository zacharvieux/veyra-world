Solvarra

Solvarra is a southern human-dominant kingdom where the Crown and the Church of Aureon are deeply intertwined.

It is fertile, wealthy, organized, martial, and highly structured.

The Church influences education, marriage, morality, public ceremony, religious courts, and social status. The Crown manages war, taxation, noble duties, foreign relations, and national infrastructure.

Solvarran society strongly values:

  • lineage;
  • family reputation;
  • religious standing;
  • civic obedience;
  • military service;
  • land;
  • public respectability;
  • social order.

Nonhuman communities exist, especially in trade cities and border regions, but can face heavier fees, guild restrictions, limitations on office, marriage restrictions, suspicion, and additional legal scrutiny.

Many outsiders experience Solvarra as severe. Many people raised inside it experience the same structure as stability, predictability, and safety.
