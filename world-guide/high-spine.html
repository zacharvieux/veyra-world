High Spine

The High Spine occupies the great central-western mountain region, including the Titan's Spine and Mount Avaran.

The High Spine occupies the great central-western mountain region, including the Titan's Spine and Mount Avaran.

It is associated with:

  • dwarven engineering;
  • mining;
  • metallurgy;
  • tunnels;
  • rail construction;
  • heavy industry;
  • mountain defense;
  • technical education.

Settlements may occupy valleys, terraces, carved stone districts, underground chambers, and major transport junctions.

Competence, reliability, safety, and craftsmanship carry strong cultural weight.
