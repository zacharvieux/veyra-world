Amber Expanse

The Amber Expanse is a vast western desert region of dunes, stone deserts, salt flats, dry basins, oasis communities, mineral districts, caravan routes, and arid coasts.

The Amber Expanse is a vast western desert region of dunes, stone deserts, salt flats, dry basins, oasis communities, mineral districts, caravan routes, and arid coasts.

Water shapes culture, infrastructure, law, hospitality, and planning.

The region is known for:

  • water engineering;
  • desalination;
  • reservoirs and managed aquifers;
  • glassmaking;
  • gems and minerals;
  • salt;
  • spices;
  • caravan trade.

Offering water to a guest carries deep cultural meaning, even where modern infrastructure has made water more accessible.
