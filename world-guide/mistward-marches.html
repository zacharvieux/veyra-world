Mistward Marches

The Mistward Marches are scattered frontier territories located where Caelora approaches the Uncrossed Mists.

The Mistward Marches are scattered frontier territories located where Caelora approaches the Uncrossed Mists.

They are not one continuous geographic region. A March may lie near desert, tundra, forest, coast, or mountain terrain.

What unites them is proximity to conditions that can disrupt:

  • navigation;
  • magic;
  • machinery;
  • communications;
  • clocks;
  • memory;
  • distance.

March culture values caution, curiosity, skepticism, redundancy, and preparation.

Important facilities maintain mechanical pumps, manual controls, conventional lighting, physical locks, paper records, and other backups alongside advanced magitech.

A common rule is:

Never trust one instrument near the Mist.
