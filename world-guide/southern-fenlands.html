Southern Fenlands

The Southern Fenlands are warm wetlands, marshes, deltas, river islands, floodplains, fisheries, and agricultural basins.

The Southern Fenlands are warm wetlands, marshes, deltas, river islands, floodplains, fisheries, and agricultural basins.

Waterways function as roads. Boats, ferries, skiffs, barges, raised walkways, levees, flood gates, and water management are ordinary parts of life.

The region is especially famous for:

  • medicine;
  • surgery;
  • magical healing;
  • pharmacology;
  • herbal medicine;
  • infectious-disease treatment;
  • fisheries;
  • fungi;
  • wetland agriculture.

Adaptation is valued more than pretending the environment can always be controlled.
