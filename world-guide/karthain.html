Karthain

A plural northern kingdom where clan traditions, mixed communities, trade, and frontier cooperation have grown together.

Karthain shares parts of the cultural world of northern Beastfolk societies without sharing Tharakai's supremacist political order.

A Mixed Northern Kingdom

Karthain is a northern mixed kingdom with strong Beastfolk cultural influence. Compared with Tharakai, it is more plural and more accustomed to daily cooperation among different peoples.

During the Age of New Crowns, mixed clans and settled communities developed stronger shared institutions. Clan tradition remains important, but trade, neighboring cultures, and practical frontier cooperation have created a distinct social environment.

Values and Reputation

Karthain commonly values honor, hospitality, keeping promises, contribution to community, martial competence, craft, and reputation.

These values overlap with some Tharakan traditions without producing the same political order. Karthain never accepted the strongest Tharakan claims of exclusive Beastfolk spiritual destiny.

Language and Faith

Karthic is historically related to Tharic but is now a distinct language. It contains strong influence from neighboring trade and cultural languages.

Varesh remains important to many people, but is not the sole faith. Religious life is more varied than in Tharakai, and local or household practices exist alongside larger traditions.

Roads and Frontier Movement

Karthain's routes include permanent roads, seasonal tracks, clan paths, and hunting corridors. Travel therefore depends on season, local knowledge, weather, and purpose rather than one uniform road network.

This mixture reflects a kingdom where settled communities, mobile traditions, hunting, livestock, and regional trade all remain important.

Economy and Food

Karthain's economy is associated with livestock, wool, timber, leather, preserved foods, metalwork, tools, hunting goods, and regional trade.

Food reflects the northern climate: livestock, preserved foods, hardy grain, roots, smoked meat, stews, hunting, and gathering all matter.

Money and Time

Karthain uses local minted silver alongside bullion, rings, livestock valuation, and foreign coin in frontier trade. Valemerian and Durakhal money is readily accepted in major markets.

Traditional contexts often use seasonal and clan dating, while Common SR appears in international law, contracts, and trade.
