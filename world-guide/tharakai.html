Tharakai

A far-northern warrior kingdom of Beastfolk clans, strong hospitality obligations, Varesh-centered faith, and an openly supremacist political order.

Tharakai is one of Aldara's most culturally distinctive northern states, but it should never be treated as representative of all Beastfolk.

Kingdom and Clans

Tharakai is a far-northern warrior kingdom dominated by Beastfolk clans. Clan, ancestry, hunting, courage, reputation, hospitality, loyalty, and military tradition carry major cultural weight.

Its warrior monarchy and clan systems became more unified during the post-Sundering Age of New Crowns, but families, clans, frontier communities, and individuals still disagree frequently about politics and identity.

The Supremacist Political Order

Tharakai maintains an openly Beastfolk-supremacist political order. Humans, elves, dwarves, Demonkin, and dissenting Beastfolk may face discrimination depending on region and circumstance.

This political ideology is not the same thing as saying every Tharakan thinks alike, and it does not define Beastfolk elsewhere in Veyra. Karthain, Caelora, Valemere, and other regions contain very different Beastfolk communities and traditions.

Hospitality and Reputation

Hospitality has serious social meaning. A guest accepted beneath a roof may gain real protection and create obligations between host and guest. Reputation, courage, loyalty, and the keeping of obligations matter strongly in a society where clan networks and difficult travel can determine who survives a crisis.

Language

Tharic is the major traditional language of Tharakai and contains many regional and clan dialects. It is historically related to Karthic, but the two are distinct after centuries of separate cultural development.

There is no universal Beastfolk language. A Beastfolk raised in another homeland may speak neither Tharic nor Karthic.

Varesh and Other Faith

Varesh-centered worship is prominent. Varesh is publicly associated with hunting, senses, animals, survival, healing, transformation, instinct, courage, and ferocity, with titles such as First Beast, Father of Claws, Thousand-Formed, Lord of the Hunt, and Blood That Runs Wild.

Varesh is not the universal god of all Beastfolk, and even within the north different lineages and communities portray Varesh differently.

Travel and Trade

Tharakai has fewer uniformly maintained highways than southern Aldaran states, but extensive local knowledge of routes through difficult northern land. Terrain knowledge, clan paths, weather, and regional experience can matter more than an official road map.

The kingdom depends heavily on trade with Durakhal. Food, livestock, hides, timber, and preserved goods move toward the mountain Holds; metal, tools, weapons, and engineered products move back.

Food, Money, and Time

Northern diets reflect cold climate, hunting traditions, livestock, preserved foods, hardy grain, roots, smoked meat, stews, and regional gathering.

Trade uses local silver alongside bullion, rings, livestock valuation, and foreign coin. In remote clan territories, useful goods may matter more than distant coinage.

Traditional contexts often use seasonal and clan dating, while Common SR appears in international law and trade.
