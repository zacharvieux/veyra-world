Tharakai

Tharakai is a far-northern warrior kingdom dominated by Beastfolk clans and strongly influenced by Varesh.

Tharakai is a far-northern warrior kingdom dominated by Beastfolk clans and strongly influenced by Varesh.

Clan, ancestry, hunting, courage, reputation, hospitality, loyalty, and military tradition carry major cultural weight.

Tharakai also maintains an openly Beastfolk-supremacist political order. Humans, elves, dwarves, Demonkin, and dissenting Beastfolk may face discrimination depending on region and circumstance. This ideology does not mean every Tharakan thinks the same way; clans, frontier communities, families, and individuals disagree frequently.

Hospitality carries serious social meaning. A guest accepted beneath a roof may gain real protection and create obligations between host and guest.

Tharakai depends heavily on trade with Durakhal: food, livestock, hides, timber, and preserved goods move one direction, while metal, tools, weapons, and engineered products move the other.

Varesh-centered worship is prominent, but Tharakai does not define every Beastfolk culture in Veyra.
