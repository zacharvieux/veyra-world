Dawning Coast

The Dawning Coast is Caelora's great maritime gateway.

The Dawning Coast is Caelora's great maritime gateway.

It contains major ports, shipyards, naval institutions, river mouths, merchant districts, international communities, and the strongest regular contact with Tsukara and Aldaran trade.

The region is associated with:

  • Stonewake construction;
  • naval power;
  • shipping;
  • international commerce;
  • customs;
  • communications;
  • shipbuilding;
  • foreign communities.

A major public political debate concerns how far Caelora should extend permanent facilities and military protection beyond its own shores.
