Aldara is politically fragmented, culturally diverse, and deeply shaped by distance.

People often identify strongly with kingdom, Hold, clan, town, faith, guild, profession, or family. Borders can be political claims rather than perfectly agreed lines. Different maps may disagree about grazing rights, forests, river access, old fortifications, and ancestral holdings.

Most of Aldara operates at an advanced medieval technological level, supplemented by magic, imported Caeloran devices, sophisticated engineering, and strong local craft traditions.

No Aldaran realm is completely self-sufficient. Trade is one of the major reasons old rivals continue speaking to one another.
