Valemere

Valemere is the great multicultural commercial crossroads of central Aldara.

Valemere is the great multicultural commercial crossroads of central Aldara.

It is closely connected to Caelora, the Elderwood, Karthain, Ardaren, Durakhal, Tsukara, and the Shattered Isles through trade, finance, shipping, diplomacy, scholarship, and travel.

Ancestry often matters less in Valemerian urban life than:

  • reputation;
  • profession;
  • guild;
  • city;
  • wealth;
  • civic belonging;
  • commercial relationships.

Valemere is famous for banking, merchant finance, shipping, contracts, insurance, legal services, books, hospitality, and international exchange.

There is no single dominant national faith. Its cities and ports support a wide range of religious traditions.

Valemere is often described as tolerant and flexible. Critics may call the same qualities opportunistic or excessively commercial.
