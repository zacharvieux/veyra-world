# Veyra Public Lore Additions Log

**Branch:** `world-guide-build`  
**Purpose:** Record public-facing lore created during the authorized website completion pass.  
**Status:** These additions are present on the preview branch for review. They should be treated as approved public canon once the website pass is accepted/merged, unless Zach explicitly revises them.

## 2026-09-13 — Website Completion Pass

### Northern Reach — First Winter
Added **First Winter**, a common Northern Reach custom marking the first full winter completed by a child, newcomer, or newly independent household in a community. The practice is deliberately practical: useful gifts, preparation, and belonging rather than a universal religious or legal adulthood rite.

**Reason added:** The public source established strong Northern Reach preparedness culture but did not provide a named everyday custom. The Website Manifest specifically identified First Winter as an appropriate regional custom if approved.

**Published on:** `world-guide/northern-reach.html`

### Southern Fenlands — First Crossing
Added **First Crossing**, a widespread Fenland custom in which a young person is trusted to complete a familiar local water crossing with increasing independence. It emphasizes current, weather, rescue procedure, local right-of-way, and knowing when not to cross.

**Reason added:** The public source strongly establishes waterways as roads and regional safety culture but lacked a named custom expressing that relationship.

**Published on:** `world-guide/southern-fenlands.html`

### Dawning Coast — First Horizon
Added **First Horizon**, a coastal custom marking the first time someone travels beyond sight of their home harbor. It may be observed by sailor families, merchants, naval cadets, migrants, researchers, and other coastal communities.

**Reason added:** The public source establishes the Dawning Coast as Caelora's maritime gateway but lacked a small public custom showing how ordinary people experience departure and sea travel.

**Published on:** `world-guide/dawning-coast.html`

### Mistward Marches — Marcher Rule
Added the **Marcher Rule**: a mission-critical system should have more than one independent way to function, and at least one backup should not depend on magic or saltstone.

Common phrasing:
> **If you cannot afford to lose one method, you do not have enough methods.**

**Reason added:** This formalizes the already-established March culture of redundancy, mundane backups, manual controls, paper records, and skepticism near the Uncrossed Mists.

**Published on:** `world-guide/mistward-marches.html`

### Shattered Isles — Harbor Board
Added the **harbor board**, a common but not universal practice in established Shattered Isles ports. Posted notices identify current authority, harbor fees, quarantine rules, salvage procedures, prohibited flags or vessels, pilotage requirements, emergency signals, and temporary closures.

**Reason added:** The public source repeatedly establishes that law, authority, salvage rights, and welcomed flags change harbor by harbor. The harbor board gives travelers a grounded public-facing way to navigate that reality without inventing a unified island government.

**Published on:** `world-guide/shattered-isles.html`

## Integration Notes

The remainder of the website expansion in this pass primarily **integrates already-established public information** from `Veyra_Player_Public_Source.md` across regional pages. It does not count as newly invented lore merely because material was consolidated from separate sections such as Daily Life, Travel, Technology, Calendar, Currency, Faiths, or History.

No GM-only cosmology, hidden faction truth, hidden sector anomaly, Elderwood interior cartography, Hallowstone secret, false-god truth, or other restricted material was promoted into the public site during this pass.
